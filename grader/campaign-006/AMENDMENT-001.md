# Prospective construction detail

Accepted before source sealing or measurements on 2026-11-10.

In concentrated8 and concentrated12, candidate i (i = 0,1,2) obtains protein and
ligand models by applying binary-coordinate gauge flips with masks 2i+1 and 2i+2
to the positive chain model. This preserves each denominator partition while
producing distinct full factor-table identities. The purpose is to distinguish
refinement savings from byte-identical shared-target reuse. In shared12 every
candidate instead uses the same complete protein and ligand targets.

The generator seal records exact random-seed offsets and all executable details
before any measured results. These choices may not be tuned using outcomes.
