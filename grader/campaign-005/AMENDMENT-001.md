# Prospective amendment 001: model-transfer mechanistic pilot

This amendment follows grader freeze `546cc5` and precedes any new molecular prediction. It does not alter the canonical inputs, WT normalization, metadata-only eligibility/duplicate selection, caps or complete-denominator requirements. Exact activation remains dependent on a validated source decoder, conformer/sequence mapping, fixed slot manifest and relevant synthetic checks. No numerical outcome prompted this amendment.

The primary direct matched-assay gate is not established. The published retrospective labels include heterogeneous biological contexts. Independently inspected prepared coordinates also retain Cys118 in Ras and Cys81 in the modeled Raf segment, whereas the 2020 BLI methods describe engineered C118S and C81I/C96M assay backgrounds. The model's 215 coordinate residues are amino acids, with no explicit nucleotide/metal entries; an active-state structural proxy does not supply those molecules. Missing/different background and model approximations must remain visible. The 2020 table alone cannot prove an exact assay match.

The actual full-precision rigid input archive nevertheless permits a useful **model-transfer mechanistic pilot**. Its primary question is whether and by how much an ensemble changes the within-model mutation effects compared with the same model's single minimum-energy conformation. This is an analysis of a declared discrete approximation and its transfer to heterogeneous existing observations, not new experimental validation or a discovery of a better binder.

For every eligible variant, compute all three matched mutant and WT rigid state quantities `Emin`, `logZ`, Cartesian count and finite-support count. Report the rigid GMEC binding score `-beta*(Emin_C-Emin_P-Emin_L)` and the ensemble binding score `logZ_C-logZ_P-logZ_L`; normalize both to that variant's own configuration WT before comparing different designs.

Define the state ensemble correction as `A = logZ + beta*Emin = log(sum exp(-beta*(E-Emin)))`. For a nonempty finite target, `0 <= A <= log(Nfinite)` up to declared numerical error. Its signed binding correction is `A_C-A_P-A_L`, and the mutation-level correction subtracts the corresponding WT value. This quantity is a log effective-conformer-count correction, **not Shannon entropy**. A claim about configurational entropy instead requires the separate expectation `beta*mean(E)+logZ` and its own numerical validation.

Primary outputs are the complete distribution of mutation-level ensemble corrections and every resolved change in the predicted direction of binding effect relative to matched WT. Secondary outputs are pairwise variant-order differences between the WT-normalized ensemble and GMEC predictions, with ties/uncertainty retained. Use absolute score/normalized-score agreement tolerance `1e-8` per independent state score and `1e-7` for a reported mutation-effect or method-difference sign. A value within the latter allowance is unresolved, not an improvement/decrease. An ordering pair needs a gap above the sum of its two mutation-effect allowances. If a computed reference cannot support those allowances, mark the affected comparisons unresolved; do not force a sign from extra displayed digits.

Independently reproduce all feasible admitted state quantities using a decoder and summation path that do not import experiment code. Recompute at least the smallest complete admitted WT/mutant three-state record at both 60 and 90 decimal digits from exact binary64 source energy values, with gas constant/temperature semantics stated. High-precision agreement is numerical evidence; it is not automatically an outward-rounded certificate. Preserve genuine `+infinity` constraints and reject NaN/negative infinity or unsupported scalar/reference conventions. Never create source-weight zeros merely by exponentiating a finite energy in binary64.

Archived BBK*/EWAK* bounds are previously published different-model comparators, matched to exact source configuration and WT when available. They are not rigid-target references. Record missing/unusable rows and overlapping bound-normalized effects. The parent and source worker have viewed some published computational columns/ranks in the S2 transcription before this amendment. The grader has read the paper's aggregate claims but not new rigid scores. This pilot is not blind to the published literature; the metadata-only input family/selection rule was already frozen, and no changes follow observed rigid outcomes.

An empirical comparison is descriptive and stratified by verified original isoform/species, nucleotide/background, assay source and observation type. Label each stratum as direct match, declared transfer, or unresolved. Unresolved biological metadata does not silently become a matched context. Where enough homogeneous observations remain, report the previously specified rank statistics with all exclusions and uncertainty. Pooled mixed-context ranks may be contextual only. No phenotype-level improvement is established merely by a direction/order difference, correlation or native-score agreement.

The primary mechanistic result can be positive, null or unresolved. Correct nontrivial ensemble corrections or resolved method-dependent decisions would identify where counting matters in this model; uniformly negligible corrections or no decision changes would delimit its practical contribution here. Neither outcome supports a general solver ranking, ETH claim or biologically superior method. The rounded-energy sensitivity fallback is not activated in this study.
