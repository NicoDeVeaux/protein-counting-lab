## Numerical and mechanistic result

Before reading worker score values, the grader enumerates every admitted source target using **exact integer-scaled dyadic energies**, then Decimal 90-digit weights/logarithms. An explicit exact-energy comparison omits terms only at gaps of at least 80 RT, bounding their total log-partition contribution by `N_omitted * exp(-80)` because the shifted partition sum includes a minimum-energy term of weight one. The largest such bound is below **8.07e-30**. This is an independently bounded numerical reference, not a directed-rounding certificate for a physical model. RT is the exact binary64 value of the archived source expression `1.9891/1000.0 * 298.15`.

All **78 state results** pass the frozen allowances. Maximum absolute errors are **1.59e-14 in log Z**, **2.00e-14 in GMEC log weight**, and **1.20e-14 in the state ensemble correction**. All **13 normalized query results** pass, with maximum error below **1.39e-14**. Separate 60/90-digit passes for all six states of the smallest complete N71R/ks28 record differ by less than **5.10e-59**. Every mathematical conformation weight is positive in this target; finite support equals the full conditioned Cartesian count.

The worker records **3,012,982 exponential underflows** during floating arithmetic. These are positive numerical-tail contributions, not source hard zeros, excluded conformations or exact support pruning. Comparison against the independently bounded positive-target references verifies their aggregate score effect within the declared tolerances. The observed `longdouble` mantissa field is **52 bits** on this host: the implementation must not be described as receiving extra extended precision merely from that type name.

The mutation-level ensemble correction ranges from **−1.377494** to **+0.428560** natural-log units. It is a difference of log effective-conformer-count corrections, not Shannon entropy. All 13 effects retain the same resolved sign under GMEC and ensemble scoring. All 78 pairwise comparisons resolve: **76 preserve the order and two reverse it**.

## Biological interpretation

The released model and observations do not provide a direct matched molecular assay. In particular, prepared coordinates retain Ras Cys118 and modeled Raf Cys81, whereas the 2020 BLI methods describe engineered C118S and C81I/C96M backgrounds; no explicit nucleotide/metal is listed among the 215 prepared amino-acid residues. Historical source observations also involve different Ras/assay contexts. These are declared model approximations/transfer differences, not an experimentally validated identity mapping. Absolute standard-state calibration is not established. A full-precision matrix is not by itself a calibrated physical affinity model.

The frozen analysis properly separates **eight Fridman relative-binding observations** from **five S2 rows with Kd values whose original row-specific assay assignment remains unresolved**. The latter are listed without pooled inference. There is no 13-point combined biological correlation and no conversion of off-rate, percent radioactivity or enrichment into Kd. Missing measurement errors do not become zero error.
