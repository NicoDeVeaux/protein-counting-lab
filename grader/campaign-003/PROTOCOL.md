# Campaign 003: independent grading protocol

Exploratory local CPU research (`poc`), frozen by adjacent `FREEZE.json`. Question: does **exact hard-zero constraint propagation**, followed by the same exact algebraic simplifier, improve the usable conventional counting regime or its cost on all six published CPD targets? Compare baseline **simplified** against **hardzero-pruned+simplified**. No preferred winner, successful speedup, or new algorithm is required for a useful result.

Fresh context is procedural separation, not a guarantee against bias.

No campaign003 implementation, derived dataset, preprocessing result, or measurement is read to write this protocol. The grader freezes first and then pauses until the parent supplies a sealed bundle. The parent freezes the complete static execution manifest, exact trial list/order, implementations, controls, references, numerical/resource contracts, and environment before campaign003 target preprocessing or measurement. All preparation/pilots and their exposure enter a ledger. No outcome-selected cohort, resource change, reference upgrade, window selection, or silent retry is permitted. Later corrections require numbered dated hashed amendments, exposure disclosure, and retained earlier rows/artifacts. Existing frozen files remain unchanged.

Adjacent schemas inherit campaign002's field contract; only the manifest's campaign literal changes to `003`. Additional support, phase-clock and block fields must be specified in the execution manifest and independently audited even where the inherited schema permits extra properties. Schema acceptance alone is not scientific acceptance.

Bind every original model byte to campaign002's original execution manifest and hashes recorded in `FREEZE.json`. Archive raw-source provenance and reuse the audited `Etmp + BKB + pair` convention; do not add SELF again. These are single-state beta1 numerical models, separate from controls and experimental affinity data.

The target remains the ideal-real partition sum induced by stored binary64 log-factor values and declared offsets. At conversion, only the author literal sentinel `1.0E38` maps to a hard zero. During propagation **only literal `-Infinity` entries are zero**. Every finite log entry, however negative or extreme, retains positive mass. Energy cutoffs, epsilon thresholds, exponentiation underflow, and tolerance-based support decisions are forbidden in an exact same-target arm. NaNs, malformed domains, missing factors or unsupported conversions are failures.

## Support and algebra gate

Require checkable original-domain mappings, retained/deleted original state IDs, per-stage domain sizes, factor slicing/axis maps, elimination order, entry counts, represented graph summaries, and named allocation estimates for both arms. Preserve every variable's multiplicity when removing a factor or variable. The baseline must perform the same algebraic simplifier without support propagation; existing campaign002 simplified artifacts may establish identity but cannot supply free baseline preprocessing in a cold comparison.

The grader independently rebuilds support from original factors without importing experiment preprocessing. A deletion is sound only if a retained-domain factor has no finite supporting tuple for that state, or a separately checked exact hard-zero argument proves all its completions have zero mass. Verify the deletion sequence or reconstruct the declared deterministic fixed point; inspect all original selected factor entries and every mapping. Retained assignments map injectively back to original assignments with the same factor values; every removed assignment has zero weight. Locally supported states need not extend to a globally positive assignment. Local consistency must never be reported as complete support enumeration.

An empty domain or identically zero factor proves `Z=0`, and must yield explicit exact-zero handling with `log Z=-Infinity`; retained nonempty domains do not prove `Z>0`. Inconsistent positive-looking cycles are a required control. An already singleton domain, fully positive model, disconnected variable, scalar factor and all-fixed case must preserve their meaning. Do not count surviving domain-product size as the number of positive-support assignments.

After slicing, check each claimed exact singleton/constant/separable rewrite using exact identities of the stored binary-rational log values (for example independent `Fraction` arithmetic), all domain multiplicities, scalar terms and restoration offsets. Rounding-close rank/separability is insufficient. Audit the composition, not just each isolated transform. Where feasible, independent enumeration/high precision checks original, baseline and pruned representations. Else retain algebraic proof plus the stated numerical uncertainty; matching two experiment solvers is not an oracle.

## Methods, budgets and clocks

The proposed compared paths are existing local log-space VE and batched enumeration. The established pyagrum comparator is optional if lightweight and frozen before execution; use the corrected adapter v2, pin actual package/build/configuration, verify its quota units, and disclose its conditional-query reconstruction. Linear-weight conversion must reject loss of finite positive entries rather than changing the target. Distinguish comparison of two representations within a method from claims comparing solver algorithms. Unsupported established conversion is retained evidence, not proof of intrinsic impossibility.

The parent must predeclare a finite total campaign wall budget, finite per-row wall limits (at most 300 seconds), per-row preprocessing limits, named table/allocation policy, enumeration-state policy, total worker/descendant RSS threshold and enforcement/observed-only semantics. A single common row watchdog must include loading, propagation/simplification, solver ingestion, inference and valid output. Charge preprocessing failures and budget-exhausted/skipped rows. Preserve watchdog sample interval, trigger/kill timing, overshoot and separate lifetime high-water scope. Named table caps and heuristic workspace estimates are not total RSS ceilings or conservative bounds without proof.

Record monotonic wall and CPU phase clocks for original model load/parse, support propagation, algebraic simplification/order selection, solver ingestion/conversion, inference and valid-result serialization. Record measured preprocessing separately **and include it in end-to-end cost for each arm**, starting from the same original model boundary. Include startup/external monitoring as separately named scopes if excluded from the scientific endpoint. If persistent artifacts are reused, disclose their full production/serialization cost and report cold and amortized scopes separately; a kernel-only gain is not an end-to-end gain. End-to-end memory is the peak of the whole pipeline, not a sum or a kernel-only relabeling of lifetime high-water.

Freeze machine/OS, package versions, threads, cache/warmup policy, concurrency, seeds and exact schedule. Use at least three fresh-process repeats per paired feasible cell, one timed worker at a time, paired arm blocks with balanced/randomized order and a frozen seed. Preserve trial start/end timestamps, block IDs and arm order. Keep common methods/resources/accuracy matched. Static resource rejections need no unsafe allocation; every planned repeat still needs an identity/status. No late extra repeats, favorable order windows or erased first attempts.

## Audit and judgment

Seal source/derived models, mapping/support/algebra evidence, ledger, full implementations/wrappers, environment/commands, exact trial matrix, raw stdout/stderr/records and hash inventory. Grader independently checks all hashes, freeze chronology and exact planned-to-observed reconciliation. Each row keeps model/arm/method/profile/repeat/block identity, output and a distinct status: validated success, unverified accuracy, exact zero, numerical/wrong answer, unsupported conversion, static table/state skip, allocation error, RSS kill, timeout, signal/crash, infrastructure error, or total-budget skip. Exact-zero completion counts as correct only with independent proof. Missingness and every original retry remain visible.

Primary evidence is all-six case-level semantic correctness, reduction size, completion/failure outcomes, preprocessing and end-to-end cost/RSS. Compare matched correct completed cells; show absolute values, medians, full repeat ranges, paired ratios, censoring and denominator. Do not turn a failed baseline into an infinite speedup. Separate published models, synthetic mechanisms and numerical controls. Correlated repeats are not independent proteins.

Assess repeat dispersion and early/middle/late block stability for both phase and end-to-end clocks. Timing superiority requires repeat-consistent direction and a margin that survives the observed ranges and order/schedule variation. Unstable/drifting observations block a superiority/crossover verdict, even with a favorable median; retain feasibility/memory and descriptive costs. Do not attribute drift causally without telemetry. Do not delete early rows, timeouts or short timings. Kernel, preprocessing and end-to-end conclusions remain separate.

Judge gates separately: (1) integrity/prospectivity, (2) same-target support/algebra/numerics, (3) all-six coverage and retained failures, (4) resources and fair measurement, (5) interpretation/usefulness. A sound no-op, limited pruning, new obstruction, verified negative result, or unresolved timing result may be useful. A correctness failure disqualifies affected same-target speed claims regardless of speed. Report audited coverage and residual uncertainty. No conventional inference result establishes ETH implementation, a novel counting algorithm, improved affinity prediction, biological validity, or general solver superiority. Publication remains outside authorization.
