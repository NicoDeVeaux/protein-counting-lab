**Useful conventional feasibility evidence, with verified artifacts and a disclosed adapter correction.** The study identifies a usable local regime, representation costs, and numerical conversion limits. It supports neither a broad solver ranking nor an ETH or biological-prediction claim. No winner is required for this result to be informative.

The author energy convention is **Etmp + BKB + pair**, without adding SELF again. Only literal `1.0E38` becomes a hard zero. All other finite energies remain finite logs. The new models are arbitrary fixed-AA, **single-state beta=1 numerical targets**; they are not affinity measurements, physical-temperature predictions, paired binding ensembles or sequence optimization.

## Resource findings that survive the correction

The low/high profiles enforce named **16/128 MiB table policies**, **128/512 MiB process-RSS thresholds**, and **10/20-second wall limits**. The sampled 10 ms watchdog and worker lifetime high-water are separately scoped; they do not guarantee an instantaneous RSS ceiling. No measured row is classified as an RSS-limit failure.

The original adapter followed a misleading installed Python docstring. Official version-pinned C++ [forwarding code](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/agrumery/aGrUM/3.3.2/src/agrum/base/graphicalModels/inference/scheduler/scheduledInference_inl.h) and [unit conversion](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/agrumery/aGrUM/3.3.2/src/agrum/base/graphicalModels/inference/scheduler/scheduler_inl.h) independently confirm MiB. Passing **1** imposed a **1 MiB native scheduler quota**. Thus the fifteen `std::bad_alloc` rows were not evidence of the declared host-RSS limits. Version 2 passes 1024 MiB while preserving the external limits; all fifteen cases recover. Outcome-selected diagnostics remain separate from both campaigns.

The **87 custom join rejections** and **21 native-wrapper clique rejections** are allocation-policy decisions, not observed RAM exhaustion. All **72 positive-entry underflow** and **six roundtrip-tolerance** rejections independently reproduce. They correctly prevent silent target changes, but establish a limitation of this linear-weight adapter rather than impossibility of the counting problem. The established comparator is pyagrum 3.3.2 Shafer-Shenoy inference through **one conditional-marginal query per variable**, not a native single-query partition-function benchmark.

The larger published models retain complete interactions among **10–25 nontrivial variables** after singleton/constant reductions. No sparse larger published family was obtained. Five have recorded dense-join predictions above 512 MiB; this does not lower-bound other algorithms. Exact separable/constant controls demonstrate algebraic simplification mechanisms only in the labeled synthetic cohort.

For 1FYN, both representations have the same **126,443,520-byte** largest table. Corrected high-profile VE nevertheless records **455–496 MB** whole-process peak RSS for the original representation and **207–208 MB** after simplification. This is a useful local memory observation. The static workspace estimate is not an upper bound: its **252,905,192 bytes** are below the kernel's own **268,710,632-byte** live-array estimate. Keep named tables, workspace estimates and total process RSS distinct. Offline preparation timing excludes artifact serialization and raw-source preparation; it is not full source-to-result latency.

## Why there is no broad timing verdict

The corrected run is strongly nonstationary. Among **167 cells with three completed repeats**, wall-time max/min has median **3.35** and maximum **16.28**; **104 cells exceed twofold** and **56 exceed fivefold**. Kernel max/min has median **2.26**, with some ratios dominated by very short timings. For unchanged custom methods and matched completed trial identities, corrected/original median wall ratios by schedule quarter are **6.06, 1.41, 1.09 and 0.89**. Kernel and CPU ratios also drift. The record does not establish the cause.

These observations defeat a clean aggregate superiority or stable crossover claim. Report case-level costs, completion counts, censoring and memory, while keeping kernel and wall scopes separate. Do not discard early trials, replace timeouts or choose a later favorable schedule window after seeing it. Three repeats do not repair this drift.
