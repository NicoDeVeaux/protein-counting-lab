## Gate 1: what the source target means

Independently parse original archived metadata and factor tables, without importing experiment conversion/preprocessing. For all 14 cases establish selector IDs/order, residue IDs and amino-acid labels, conformer mappings, state membership, table index convention, scalar terms, source decimal values, and source/derived hashes. Prove that the same declared sequence conditions C, P and L and that absent selectors are handled by explicit state membership rather than accidental index reuse.

Independently distinguish selector-consistency zeros, energy-table literal zeros, positive terms, DUMMY conformers/labels, and UFO or other auxiliary/underflow encodings. Establish the construction's meaning from source evidence, including whether auxiliary terms are physical conformers, summed nuisance states, constraints, reciprocal weights, or numerical devices. Do not infer physical Boltzmann semantics just from a filename or a successful decoder. Identify possible source-generation underflow separately from later adapter underflow; absent recoverable energy data, a source zero cannot be restored to a positive value by guessing.

Verify state-specific factor assignment, signs, temperature/beta and units, normalization/energy shifts and every sequence-dependent scalar or offset. Demonstrate algebraically whether the intended score is `logZ_C - logZ_P - logZ_L` for these extracted states and how normalization constants are restored. Same-source decimal weights define the represented computational target; recovering an unrounded physical model is a separate task. If physical interpretation or compatible state normalization cannot be established, report a gate failure and restrict any arithmetic to explicitly named source-encoding diagnostics. Such diagnostics do not become an affinity or physical sequence-ranking result. Correct arithmetic cannot repair an invalid decomposition.

Conditioning, algebraic simplification and optional hard-zero propagation must preserve that same target. Only source-represented exact zeros may remove support; every finite represented log weight remains positive. Verify original-state maps, deletion witnesses/fixed point, duplicate-scope support alignment, all retained numeric/scalar terms and domain multiplicity. Local consistency does not establish global feasibility. Report Cartesian products separately from positive-support counts. A dummy singleton representing proven zero mass is not a feasible witness.

## Gate 2: independent references and numerics

The preparation manifest fixes exhaustive reference enumeration using an independently implemented raw-source-aware decoder, Decimal precision 90 and a second pass at 120 digits, per-target assignment/wall/memory caps and total cap before any conditioning/inference. Enumerate all predeclared targets in fixed order under the same policy. Preserve incomplete, unsupported and cap-exceeded references. Do not omit a variant or switch to an easier oracle based on its result. Method agreement is not an independent reference. No uncapped recovery computation is required by this rubric.

References must account for source decimals versus stored rounded logarithms and quantify any conversion difference. Exact zero requires a combinatorial support proof or complete enumeration with no positive assignment, not a rounded small sum. Nonzero references require both precision passes to agree within `1e-10` log units, with any residual source/conversion discrepancy disclosed. Precision agreement is numerical evidence, not an outward-rounded interval certificate. A reference that cannot support the frozen tolerance remains unresolved.

For each reference-covered finite log partition, accept absolute error at most `1e-8` log units. Require exact zero/nonzero status and restore all offsets before comparison. `Z_C=0` with both denominators positive gives `logK=-Infinity`; any zero denominator makes the ratio **undefined**, including `0/0`. Never cancel infinities or rank undefined ratios. If every ratio is undefined, report no ranking.

For finite logK, compare the raw, unrounded log ratio and each constituent logZ. Use the sum of the three absolute logZ tolerances, plus disclosed reference/conversion uncertainty, as the score comparison envelope. Report pairwise ordering and top-k membership for `k=1,3,5` when applicable, preserving exact known ties and numerical uncertainty. Overlapping score envelopes are unresolved, not forced ties or wrong rankings. Do not round scores before ranking or invent a deterministic ordering to improve agreement. Report coverage; a missing/undefined variant prevents an unqualified full-panel top-k claim. A separable wrong order outside the envelope is a correctness failure.

Tests must be frozen before preparation and exercise analytic finite three-state ratios; complex zero; protein/ligand zero and `0/0`; matching shared selectors with different local indices; scalar/offset restoration; a tiny positive factor; DUMMY/UFO handling; pruning multiplicity; and exact, near and resolvable rank ties. Independent phase2 audit rechecks semantics and numerics on sealed files without using implementation preprocessing.

The seal inventories full hashes of original source archives, source manifest, chosen implementation/tests, both experiment freezes, preparation/ref logs, all targets/maps/witnesses, schedule and raw trial artifacts. Store full identities in evidence; use six-character hashes in human-facing text. Each planned row has unique campaign/case/sequence/state/method/arm/profile/repeat identity, input/code/reference identities, planned caps, actual start/end, raw stdout/stderr/result, and exactly one status: reference-passing success, completed but unverified/incorrect, timeout, memory/resource-policy rejection, unsupported conversion, planned skip, or error. Undefined ratio is a score status, not numerical success. Every skip/failure states its rule/reason; reconcile the entire planned ledger, including preparation and references, without survivor-only summaries. A schema-free checker must fail on missing/duplicate/extra identities, hash mismatch, changed scientific choices, absent status/evidence, or success unsupported by a reference.

The independent verdict grades integrity/prospectivity, source semantics, same-target transformations, reference/numerical/rank correctness, coverage/failures, resource comparability and interpretation separately. A failed upstream gate blocks downstream claims, not disclosure of valid diagnostics. Retain all14 source-audit results even if the pilot stops. Final delivery includes a concise report, machine-readable verdict, independent checks and audit seal.
