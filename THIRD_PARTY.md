# Source and data rights

The root MIT license applies to original project software. It does not replace upstream licenses or grant rights over other authors' work. Original project research reports and generated result data may be reused under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), with attribution to NicoDeVeaux and the original scientific sources where applicable. Upstream numerical observations retain their source terms.

| Material | Source and treatment |
|---|---|

| Armadillo pocket affinities | Ayyildiz M et al. (2025), [paper](https://doi.org/10.1002/jcc.70160), CC BY 4.0. Numeric Table S1 transcription and provenance retained; paper/supplement archives excluded. |

| Other paper/software snapshots | Excluded; refer to the recorded original URLs, citations and checksums. Merely being publicly downloadable was not treated as a license grant. |

The bounded rights inventory is not a claim that the project owns the underlying biological measurements, models, algorithms or third-party code. Preserve source attribution and consult the original terms for your intended use.
